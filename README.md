EXN.3.2. CREATE 3D MODEL FROM ORTHOGRAPHIC PROJECTION

AIM:
To create a 3D model from given orthographic projection.

REQUIREMENTS:
 * Autodesk Fusion 360
 * Windows 10
 * 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommended 1 GB or more)
 * 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload
   
PROCEDURE:
STEP 1: Study the Given Drawing
 * Carefully observe the given engineering drawing.
 * Identify the three views: Top View, Front View and Side View.
 * Note all the important dimensions, circular portions, holes and curved surfaces.
 * Understand the shape and arrangement of the component.
 * Select a suitable drawing scale according to the drawing sheet.
 * 
Step 2: Draw the Front View
 * Draw a horizontal reference line for the Front View.
 * Mark the overall length as 420 mm using the selected scale.
 * Draw the base portion with a thickness of 30 mm.
 * Mark the centre of the object and draw a vertical centre line.
 * Construct the semicircular upper portion using a radius of 110 mm.
 * Draw the central rectangular opening with a width of 120 mm.
 * Complete the Front View by drawing all the visible outlines.
 * Add centre lines and hidden lines wherever necessary.

Step 3: Draw the Top View
 * Draw the Top View above the Front View according to the given arrangement.
 * Project the necessary vertical construction lines from the Front View.
 * Mark the overall width as 150 mm.
 * Draw the rounded ends using a radius of 60 mm.
 * Mark the two end-hole centres with a distance of 300 mm between them.
 * Draw two circular holes with a diameter of 40 mm (Ø40).
 * Mark the central hole and draw it with a diameter of 20 mm (Ø20).
 * Draw the remaining rectangular portions and hidden edges as shown in the figure.

Step 4: Draw the Side View
 * Draw the Side View on the right side of the Front View.
 * Project the required horizontal lines from the Front View.
 * Mark the overall width as 150 mm.
 * Mark the overall height as 110 mm.
 * Draw the rectangular outer boundary.
 * Construct the internal rectangular portion according to the given dimensions.
 * Mark the height of 60 mm.
 * Draw the necessary hidden lines and centre lines.

Step 5: Finishing the Drawing
 * Darken all the visible object outlines.
 * Draw hidden edges using dashed lines.
 * Draw centre lines using thin chain lines.
 * Add the necessary dimensions, arrows and extension lines.
 * Erase unwanted construction lines.
 * Check the alignment and accuracy of all three views.
